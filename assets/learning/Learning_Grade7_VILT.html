What problem does the reader face when reading an informational/news text like these?

A non-fiction/news text can contain reported information alongside claims, viewpoints, interpretations, and information from which the reader draws an inference. The reader therefore needs to examine who says what, what the text establishes, and what conclusion is supported by the evidence.

How do we read through that problem?

Keep in mind: Use the text to support your conclusion.
1. Identify source 2. Examine wording 3. Distinguish report / claim / inference 4. Use textual evidence 5. Reach a conclusion
