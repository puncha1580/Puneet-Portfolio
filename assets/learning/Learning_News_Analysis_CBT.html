Assignment

Read the two news reports

You have already read and discussed News Analysis. Now use the same reading process with two new news reports.

As you read, keep asking:

  • What information is being reported?
  • Who is giving the information?
  • What is the problem?
  • What solution or response is given?
  • What is stated, and what can I infer?
Reading 1

School starts a rainwater project

School starts a rainwater project

Pune, 18 July

Students of Shantiniketan School in Pune have started a rainwater project in their school. During the monsoon, rainwater from the roof used to flow into the street.

The school has now placed pipes to carry the rainwater into a storage tank. The stored water will be used for watering plants and cleaning the school ground.

“We wanted to make better use of the rainwater we receive,” said the school principal. Students from the Eco Club helped teachers check the pipes and keep the tank clean.

Keep in mind: First understand what the report tells us.
Reading 2

Students collect plastic for recycling

Students collect plastic for recycling

Nashik, 6 August

Students of Vidya Mandir School in Nashik have started collecting used plastic bottles from their homes and neighbourhood.

The school says that the bottles will be sent to a recycling centre. The students have also put collection boxes near the school gate.

“This will make our neighbourhood completely free of plastic waste,” said one student.

A local waste worker, however, said that collecting bottles was useful but would not solve the whole problem. He said that people also needed to reduce the use of plastic and put waste in the correct place.

Keep in mind: Notice who says each piece of information.
