FEATURED PROJECT

Fuel Dispenser User Manual

A sample manual developed for a user-documentation assignment during an interview process in 2012.

USER MANUAL · PROCEDURAL CONTENT7 PAGES · SAMPLE PROJECT

Helping station operators and customers use a fuel dispenser safely

The manual introduces the dispenser, explains the operating sequence, and provides safety guidance for routine use and selected incident scenarios.

AudienceStation operators and customers
Content scopeSystem overview, operation, safety, daily routines
BoundaryInstallation and maintenance are outside this user manual's scope

What the sample demonstrates

  • Task sequence. Operating instructions are organised around the process of dispensing fuel.
  • Safety at the point of use. Safety instructions and accident scenarios address risks during forecourt operations.
  • Defined audience and scope. The manual distinguishes routine user tasks from work intended for authorised technicians.
  • Operational coverage. Start-up, activity during operation, and closing routines are included.
