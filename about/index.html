ABOUT ME

Clarity is not decoration. It is direction.

Whether drafting hardware safety manuals, designing gamified language simulations, or structuring corpora for LLM systems, my core craft remains identical: identifying cognitive friction and removing it.

← Back to portfolio home

PHILOSOPHY

Helping people make the next right move.

Complex systems often fail their users not because the underlying technology is broken, but because the path forward is obscure. When documentation is dense or training is disorganized, people hesitate, make errors, or disengage.

I approach technical communication not as passive recording, but as active user-advocacy. Good documentation and meaningful instructional design should create certainty and accelerate autonomy.

APPROACH

Three foundational tenets

Principles refined across two decades of collaboration with engineering, product, and operations teams.

01 / Scaffolding

Cognitive Ergonomics

Respecting human working memory limits. By chunking complex concepts into discrete, progressive steps (Miller's 7±2), information becomes digestible rather than overwhelming.

02 / Usability

Action-Oriented Architecture

Users rarely read technical material for entertainment; they read to accomplish a task. Every manual, API reference, or VILT lesson must be oriented toward measurable outcomes.

03 / Adaptability

Continuous Expansion

The medium evolves—from printed manuals to DITA CCMS, interactive browser games, and retrieval-augmented LLM datasets. The core discipline of clarity remains constant.

PERSPECTIVE

Beyond the documentation desk

Outside of enterprise technology, I explore creative writing, educational game mechanics, and language acquisition puzzles. Translating abstract rules into engaging, playful formats directly sharpens how I design serious enterprise products.

I live in Mumbai and frequently work between Mumbai and Pune, collaborating with remote and distributed product teams across time zones.
